About Auburn Closet Company

We're a locally owned custom closet company in Auburn, Alabama. We design and build storage for real homes. Not a catalog grid forced into the room. Systems sized to your space and how you use it.

Every project starts with a conversation. We come to your home, look at the space, and listen. Then we draw a layout that uses the footprint. We use 3/4" thermally fused laminate panels, soft-close drawers, and quality hardware, all backed by a limited lifetime warranty.

Our installers are local. We're a division of Wheelhouse Design Company, the design-led remodeling shop next door on Pumphrey Ave. Same shop family, closet-focused work.

Looking for Auburn University's Campus Career Closet? That's a separate AU student resource. Auburn Closet Company is a privately owned custom closet design-and-install shop at 2217 Pumphrey Ave. Walk-ins, reach-ins, pantries, and laundry rooms for homeowners and builders. Call (334) 752-2872.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do custom closets cost?

Every project is different. Price depends on size, complexity, and materials. You get a detailed quote after the free consultation. No hidden fees.

How long does the process take?

From consultation to finished install, most projects take 4-5 weeks. Install itself is usually one day for a standard closet.

What materials do you use?

We use 3/4" thermally fused laminate panels. Soft-close drawers are standard. Hardware and accessories vary by project. Limited lifetime warranty.

Do I need to empty my closet before installation?

Yes. Empty the closet before install day so we can work clean and keep your stuff safe.

Do you work with new construction?

Yes. Homeowners and builders, new construction and existing homes. If you're building, book us during framing so the layout can be planned early.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Auburn, Opelika, Dadeville, Lake Martin, Smiths Station, Salem, Phenix City, and surrounding communities in Lee County and east Alabama.
