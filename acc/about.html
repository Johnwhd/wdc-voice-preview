About Auburn Closet Company

A locally owned custom closet company in Auburn, Alabama. Storage designed and built for real homes. Systems sized to the space and how it is used. Not a catalog grid forced into the room.

Every project begins with a consultation. We visit the home, review the space, and listen. Then we draw a layout that uses the footprint. Construction uses 3/4-inch thermally fused laminate panels, soft-close drawers, and quality hardware, backed by a limited lifetime warranty.

Installers are local. Auburn Closet Company is a division of Wheelhouse Design Company, the design-led remodeling shop on Pumphrey Ave. Same shop family. Closet-focused work.

Looking for Auburn University's Campus Career Closet? That is a separate AU student resource. Auburn Closet Company is a privately owned custom closet design-and-install shop at 2217 Pumphrey Ave. Call (334) 752-2872.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do custom closets cost?

Every project is different. Price depends on size, complexity, and materials. A detailed quote follows the consultation.

How long does the process take?

From consultation to finished install, most projects take 4-5 weeks. Install itself is usually one day for a standard closet.

What materials do you use?

We use 3/4-inch thermally fused laminate panels. Soft-close drawers are standard. Hardware and accessories vary by project. Limited lifetime warranty.

Do you work with new construction?

Yes. Homeowners and builders, new construction and existing homes. For new construction, book during framing so the layout can be planned early.

What areas do you serve?

Auburn, Opelika, Dadeville, Lake Martin, Smiths Station, Salem, Phenix City, and surrounding communities in Lee County and east Alabama.
