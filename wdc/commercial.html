Commercial cabinets & casework

We provide cabinet and millwork packages as a subcontractor for commercial and light commercial work in Auburn-Opelika and nearby east Alabama.

John Blackwell runs the cabinet shop and install. We're a cabinet sub, not a project manager or GC. We build in our Auburn shop and install with our own crew, so you get one local team for casework that has to fit the plans and the schedule.

What we take on

  • Commercial and light commercial casework / cabinet packages
  • Millwork coordinated to architectural drawings and finish schedules
  • Shop drawings, fabrication, and install
  • Select laminate and durable finish packages suited to high-use environments when the spec calls for them
  • Collaboration with general contractors on sequencing and punch
