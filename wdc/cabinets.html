Custom vs. semi-custom

Full customSemi-custom
How madeBuilt from scratch in our Auburn shop to the drawingsManufacturer lines with more options than stock
Best whenOdd rooms, integrated built-ins, matching existing millworkStandard layouts where speed and price matter more
PriceHigher investment; maximum fitUsually lower than full custom

Selection follows budget, timeline, and how much the room resists a catalog box. Custom is not pushed when semi-custom is the better fit.

Process & timeline

  1. Consult and measure. Clarify scope, budget range, and custom vs. semi-custom.
  2. Design and shop drawings. Layouts, elevations, and details for approval.
  3. Material selection. Doors, finishes, and hardware, often at the showroom.
  4. Shop build. Cabinets built in Auburn.
  5. Install and punch. Local install and walkthrough before closeout.
