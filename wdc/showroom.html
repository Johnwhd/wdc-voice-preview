Design consults with Deanna

Deanna Blackwell leads interior design, material selection, and project coordination. Showroom appointments work well for:

  • Kitchen & bath remodel selections
  • Cabinet door / finish decisions
  • Whole-home finish direction alongside construction
  • Closet finish coordination with Auburn Closet Co.

Bring inspiration photos, plans if you have them, and an honest budget range. We'll tell you what's realistic.

John and the shop team are on-site for build and install questions when that's what you need.
